## Voice Family — legal questions for the pilot

**Who we are.** A voice (singing) school. Adult students (18+), **all in the US**, lessons online and in person. Lessons are recorded. We are starting a small pilot (≈10 students, 2 teachers) that adds work between lessons: short clips from the lesson, a weekly practice plan, one voice reply from the teacher to a home recording, and a free 60-second "Voice Snapshot" for new people.

**How recordings are processed today.** Lesson recording → Google Drive → transcription by **AssemblyAI** → analysis and captions by **Claude (Anthropic)** → notes in **Notion** → audio clips (our own server on Railway) → a private student web page. Captions are written by AI; in the pilot the teacher will approve every caption before a student sees it.

**What we will sell (test prices).** Voice Snapshot — free. Diagnostic lesson — $29 or $49, one-time. Starter Month — 4 lessons, $140 or $180, paid once, **nothing renews**, full refund if not happy. Later: a monthly plan for independent teachers ("Founding Coach", $29/month, first 6 weeks free).

**Decisions already made.** No speech-language pathologist on the team: health-related texts are approved by the owner and the school's methodologist. All teacher work between lessons is included in the teacher's lesson rate (no per-item pay). We do not create voiceprints, scores or talent verdicts. Nothing is published without separate consent.

### Must answer before the first recording

1. **Recording consent.** Which states in our student base require all-party consent? Teacher and student in different states — which law applies? Does a checkbox form (with timestamp and version) cover lessons and home recordings? What about other people audible at home?
2. **Vendors: AssemblyAI, Anthropic, Notion, Google Drive, Railway.** Do we need a DPA with each? Is their standard DPA / API terms enough? Can we truthfully say "your recordings are never used to train AI"? What must the student be told about third-party processing?
3. **Biometrics (Illinois BIPA, Texas, Washington, others).** Do raw voice recordings or speaker-separated transcripts count as biometric identifiers when no voiceprint is created? Where do our students live — does any of these laws apply? What written confirmations do we need from vendors?
4. **Health-related notes.** A student can tap "my voice hurts / is hoarse". Is that health data (e.g., Washington My Health My Data Act, FTC Health Breach Notification Rule)? What is the minimum we may store, and for how long?
5. **No SLP.** We tell students to see a doctor or voice specialist when something hurts, and stop singing until the teacher replies. Health texts are approved by the owner and methodologist, not a clinician. Is this enough from a liability standpoint? What disclaimer should we use ("not medical advice")?
6. **Adults only.** Is a self-declared "18 or older" checkbox enough for the pilot? What do we do if we learn a user is a minor? Under the amended COPPA, is a child's voice recording personal information, and from what date? (For a future teen/child offer.)

### Must answer before landing pages and offers go live

7. **Honest pricing and refunds.** Do state auto-renewal laws and FTC negative-option rules apply to one-time purchases? For the Founding Coach monthly plan: required disclosures, consent box, reminder timing, one-click cancel. Is "first 6 weeks free, no automatic conversion" clean?
8. **Claims and guarantee.** Are these safe as written: "Not happy after your first month? Full refund." · "Singing is a skill, not a gift." · "A real coach replies to your practice every week. No scores, no verdicts." Refund window of 7 days after the 4th lesson — acceptable? Rules for using student clips or quotes (FTC Endorsement Guides, consumer reviews rule), "results vary".
9. **Privacy notice.** Does CCPA/CPRA or another state privacy law apply at our size? What notice at collection, retention periods and deletion/export wording should we use?

### Teachers

10. **Contractor vs employee.** Teachers paid per lesson; work between lessons (replies, Snapshots, caption approval) included in the rate and capped by time limits; we log minutes only to control workload; teachers follow our safety rules. Does this create "control" that points to employment (e.g., California AB5)? What should the teacher agreement say?
11. **IP and filming.** Teacher agreement: IP assignment for recipes, clips and demo videos; talent release; what happens to materials when a teacher leaves. Filming days — separate pay or included in the rate? *(owner to decide)*
12. **Cover songs.** Students sing copyrighted songs in lessons and in private clips. Any risk for private, non-public use? What if a student wants to share a clip?

**We need from you:** a short answer to 1–6 before the pilot starts; reviewed consent form, privacy notice, refund terms and teacher agreement (drafts in `05-legal-drafts.md`).
